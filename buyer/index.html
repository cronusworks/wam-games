🏢 Business Buyer Profile

We are actively buying established businesses. If you are a broker or an owner with a business that fits the profile below, we want to hear from you — and we review every serious package, starting with the tax returns.

What we're looking for

  • Established at least 5 years and SBA-eligible.
  • SDE target: about $500,000/year. Flexible down to about $350,000 for a manager-run business with recurring revenue.
  • Valuation: 2–3× SDE (higher only when real estate is included).
  • Categories: open to any category where the numbers work. Current interest: licensed trades (plumbing, HVAC, electrical), laundromats, self-storage, and residential property management.
  • Geography: anywhere in the United States except New York and New Jersey.
  • Real estate included is a plus, not a requirement.
  • Manager-run preferred — a manager in place, or a business that can run with one. Recurring revenue preferred.

What we require to review

  • Three years of business tax returns. This is a standing requirement on every deal — broker P&Ls and listing sheets do not substitute.
  • CIM or equivalent package, and the basis of the SDE figure (owner salary and add-backs).

What we pass on

  • Business-format franchises in any form. (A branded fuel supply agreement, e.g. at a gas station, is not a franchise and is fine.)
  • A business where the owner's family holds critical roles and would leave at sale.
  • Businesses under 5 years old, or not SBA-eligible.
